Why do we have repair forms?

This form connects each repair record to photos and guest feedback, tracks impact and purchase requests, and makes it easy to upload to repairmonitor.org.

When we apply for grants we consult this data. Grants pay for tools, food, and some workshops.

Before/after photos are encouraged. Good photos may end up on our social media (ask the guest if this is ok.)

"Your name" doesn't mean the person solely (or even mostly) responsible for the repair. It's just the person responsible for the form. We often work together and don't track who did what.
